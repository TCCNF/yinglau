私隱政策 Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 5 日 · Last updated: 5 October 2026

一句講晒：「影樓」唔會收集、儲存或傳送你任何個人資料。所有相片處理都喺你部 iPhone 上完成，App 冇帳戶、冇網絡請求、冇廣告、冇分析工具、冇第三方 SDK。
In short: YingLau does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. All photo processing happens on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no network requests, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

1. 我哋會用到嘅權限

  • 相機：用嚟顯示即時預覽同拍攝相片。影像只喺裝置上處理，唔會上傳。
  • 相簿（加入相片）：用嚟將你拍攝或執好嘅相片儲存到「相片」App。
  • 相簿（讀取，1.1 版起、可選）：「相簿」分頁需要讀取你嘅相片庫，先可以按類別（人像、旅行、動物、美食等）同格式（RAW／HEIC／JPEG）幫你整理，再揀相去執相。你可以喺系統提示揀「限制取用」，只開放部分相片；亦可以完全唔授權，其他功能（拍攝、執相、從選擇器匯入）照常使用。
  • 匯入相片：當你撳「從相簿揀相」，系統會顯示 Apple 嘅相片選擇器，「影樓」只會收到你親手揀嘅相片。

2. 裝置上處理（On-device）

場景偵測（人像、美食、風景等）、拍攝建議，以及「相簿」分頁嘅相片分類，全部使用 Apple 內置嘅 Vision 框架喺裝置上運算，唔會將相片或分析結果傳送到任何伺服器。

相簿分析只會讀取相片嘅縮圖同格式資訊（唔會為咗分析而由 iCloud 下載原圖），亦唔會修改或刪除你任何相片。分析結果（每張相屬於邊個類別、係咩格式）只以一個索引檔存喺「影樓」嘅本機儲存空間，刪除 App 時會一併移除。

3. 儲存喺你裝置上嘅資料

  • 你嘅偏好設定（例如自然直出、日期印、格線）。
  • 你自訂嘅調色風格，以及你匯入嘅 .cube 色彩檔案。
  • 「相簿」分頁嘅分類索引（相片識別碼、格式、類別標籤）。唔包含相片內容。

呢啲資料只存在「影樓」嘅本機儲存空間，刪除 App 時會一併移除。我哋冇辦法存取。

4. 第三方

「影樓」唔包含任何第三方廣告、分析或追蹤工具，亦唔會同任何第三方分享資料。

5. 兒童

「影樓」適合所有年齡使用。由於我哋唔收集任何資料，亦唔會向兒童收集個人資料。

6. 政策更新

如果日後功能改變而影響私隱，我哋會更新此頁並註明日期。

7. 聯絡我哋

如有任何私隱問題，請電郵至 support@spotterhk.com。

Privacy Policy (English)

Permissions

  • Camera: used for the live preview and to take photos. Images are processed on-device and never uploaded.
  • Photos (add): used to save photos you take or edit into the Photos app.
  • Photos (read, optional, from version 1.1): the Library tab reads your photo library so it can organise photos by content (portrait, travel, animals, food…) and by format (RAW / HEIC / JPEG) and let you pick one to edit. You may choose “Limited Access” or decline entirely — camera, editing and the system picker keep working.
  • Importing: when you choose “pick from library”, Apple's system photo picker is shown and YingLau only receives the photos you select.

On-device processing

Scene detection and shooting suggestions run entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No photo or analysis result leaves your device.

The Library analysis only reads thumbnails and format metadata (it never downloads originals from iCloud for analysis) and never modifies or deletes your photos. The results (per-photo category and format) are kept as a small index file inside the app and are removed when you delete it.

Data stored on your device

Your preferences, your saved custom colour styles, any .cube colour files you import, and the Library category index (photo identifiers, format and category tags — no image content) are stored locally inside the app and are removed when you delete it. We cannot access them.

Third parties & children

YingLau contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking, and shares no data with anyone. Because we collect no data, we collect none from children.
